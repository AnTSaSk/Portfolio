::prose-lede
A developer for about ten years now. What I love is digging into a problem and designing the right solution, one that holds up over time. A bit like a good slow-cooked dish: it takes time and attention, but the result is worth it.
::

With a background made of a bit of design, a bit of project management and a fair amount of character, I'm not here just to execute. I try to **understand the real need** first, even if it means pushing back on the brief.

All those years in web agencies let me work on very different projects and gain some perspective. I spot the gaps and the loose ends early, and I anticipate them to avoid a long round of QA and back-and-forth.

What matters most to me is **quality**: a well-designed architecture, clean and reusable code, and technical debt kept in check *(yes, a project with zero debt doesn't exist)*. I also industrialize **AI-assisted development** through tightly framed workflows that I supervise (custom Claude Code skills, quality gates and so on).

On the side, a few open-source side-projects: a Discord bot in TypeScript, and a Figma plugin to keep components in sync with the designs *without tearing your hair out every time the creative changes*.
